What We Make

Our core product lines

Customised to specification for capacitor voltage transformers, VFD output filtering and substation applications.

Core Product

Electro Magnetic Unit (EMU)

Used inside capacitor voltage transformers (CVTs) — comprising high voltage reactor, compensation reactor, intermediate voltage transformer and ferro-resonance suppression circuit. SE CU copper winding, CRGO M5 grade lamination, oil cooled, IP55 enclosure, rated for 110 KV to 765 KV.

Core Product

dU/Dt Choke Filter Assembly

Installed at the output of VFDs to reduce dV/dt and harmonics, protect motor insulation and improve drive reliability. Built to order for rated current, inductance and voltage requirements, with IP00 protection and ELMEX terminal blocks.

Core Product

Transformers

Custom-wound transformers manufactured with the same in-house winding, impregnation and testing process as our EMUs — supplied as standalone units or as part of a larger assembly.

Application

Where the dU/Dt Choke Filter fits in a VFD

Installed at the output section of a Variable Frequency Drive, between the inverter and the motor, to reduce dV/dt and output harmonics and protect motor insulation.
